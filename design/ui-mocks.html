Flow

  1. Launch screen — tap End on the active banner
  2. This screen — no banner; the flash is already the subject. Duration is pre-filled from the timer
  3. Confirm end — writes the end time, then returns to the launch screen, banner cleared and ready for a new entry

Dragging the duration slider moves the end time, never the start — the start is a fact, the end is your best guess. Cancelling leaves the flash running; only Confirm writes a duration.

Notes & sketches in history

  1. Notes appear in full — quoted under the entry, clamped to two lines. A note is the reason you'd read history at all, so it is never reduced to an icon
  2. Sketches appear as themselves — a real thumbnail on the trailing edge, not a badge saying one exists
  3. Tap the thumbnail to open it full-frame with its time and note beneath
  4. Tap the row itself to reopen the end card on a flash already recorded: the same card, in editing rather than ending mode

Entries with neither stay compact — the 3:02 AM bedside entry is one line and costs nothing. The list only grows where there is something to say.

Correcting a flash after the fact is the point of the tap, not a convenience. The flash she sleeps through has no length until she is awake to give it one, and a duration typed at 3am is a guess made half asleep. So the row opens the end card seeded with what is on the record, and the note is edited in place rather than added to — the only way to fix something written in the dark. This is a PATCH, deliberately not the end endpoint: that one closes a running flash and must keep refusing anything else, or a second bedside press would silently rewrite the flash that just closed.
